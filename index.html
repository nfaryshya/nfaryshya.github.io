HISTORY, IDENTITY & INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

Historical Memory and Narrative in Malaysia–Indonesia Relations

A qualitative study of how historical narratives and collective memory shape bilateral relations, national identity and public perceptions in Malaysia and Indonesia.

MY CONTRIBUTIONQualitative case study, thematic analysis and presentation of recommendations.

How does a shared past shape present-day relations?

Examined how historical narratives and collective memory influence national identity, public perceptions and diplomatic sensitivities, including the lasting influence of Konfrontasi.

Qualitative case study & thematic analysis

Considered how political actors, media and education shape interpretations of shared historical events, then used thematic analysis to examine the connection between historical memory and diplomacy.

Supporting informed, balanced dialogue

Presented recommendations on balanced historical narratives, responsible media reporting and continued bilateral dialogue.
